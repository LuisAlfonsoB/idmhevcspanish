Live restream

Play a video live into an RTMP server (YouTube, Twitch, OK.ru…) with ffmpeg, in real time. The video is passed through untouched; closing the browser stops every stream.

Streams

1. Video

The chosen file is downloaded first (progress is shown in the stream's status), then streamed from disk.

2. Stream to

If the server rejects the stream because of the audio codec, choose Auto or Always convert. Up to 2 streams at once. Needs enough upload bandwidth for the video's bitrate.
